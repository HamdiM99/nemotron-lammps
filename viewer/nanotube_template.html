Method

How each number was obtained

  • Interlayer barriers and retraction force: rigid shells, AIREBO with its Lennard-Jones term; the core is moved step by step and LAMMPS returns the energy.
  • Charges: every atom of a tube sits at the same potential; the code solves the linear system for Gaussian charges on the atoms (atomistic moment method).
  • Electrostatic force and pull-in voltage: Coulomb force on one core from all other charges; the threshold is where it exceeds the van der Waals retraction force. It scales with the square of the voltage.
  • Telescoping oscillator: the core is pulled out and released; the measured frequency is checked against the estimate from the retraction force.
Scope

What this model does not show

  • Two walls and nm instead of ten walls and 200 nm: far less charge, so the pull-in voltage here is much higher than the ~5 V of the paper.
  • The field needed at this size is beyond what a real device would sustain. Read the trend, not the value.
  • Shells are rigid in the static scans; only the oscillator run lets the core move.
  • No rotation result. Closing runs under voltage on this reduced model (armchair and chiral pairs, up to 100 ps) did not show sustained rotation of the cores. They were removed from this page and from the repository.
